# Buyer notes — CalloutSlip

Signature: Mourad.Soltani

Prepared for a one-to-one sale conversation. Not a mass list.

## What they are buying

A single-purpose quote tool for an owner-operator who prices emergency call-outs by hand or in a notes app. The buyer is a locksmith, plumber, electrician, glazier, HVAC tech, or appliance tech who already charges a night or holiday premium and loses time rewriting the same slip.

## What is included

- Browser slip form
- Pricing engine with window multipliers, mileage, labor, parts markup, tax, deposit, and minimum charge
- Health endpoint and unit tests
- MIT license, signature Mourad.Soltani

## What is not included

- Payments, SMS, dispatch, or a customer database
- Tax advice. Rates are inputs, not a jurisdiction engine
- Hosting. The buyer runs it locally or on a small VPS

## Fit check before a call

Ask whether they already charge different rates for evening, night, weekend, and holiday, and whether the customer expects a written total before the tech leaves. If both are yes, the slip matches the workflow. If they need scheduling or invoicing, this is the wrong product.

## Suggested first price conversation

A one-time setup in the $149–$299 range, or $19/month if the buyer wants the owner to keep the rate card updated. Do not quote until the buyer confirms they send written call-out totals today.
